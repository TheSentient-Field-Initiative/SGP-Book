# Chapter 20. A Philosophical Framework, an Explanatory Ambition

**The distinction the whole book was building toward:** SGP-the-metaphysics vs. SGP-the-explanation vs. SGP-the-research-program. Four strategic reviews separated them. This chapter reports the separation.

## RD-001: clarify before researching

The reorientation disentangled five commitments — sentience-as-response, reality-as-sentience, world-as-experience-content, mathematics-as-possibility, actualization — with their dependencies (C2 needs C1; C5 needs C4; C3 needs C1+C2+C5) and named the two unexamined moves: the *category move* (property of systems → nature of reality) and the *modal move* (possibility → actuality). Classification: FOUNDATIONAL CLARIFICATION REQUIRED BEFORE RESEARCH. The package could not be tested as a unit because its parts fail differently.

## RD-002: coherent, but not distinctive

The coherence audit, with provenance tables and seven adversarial objections (all sustained or partially sustained), found the package internally consistent — and mappable onto existing traditions: Whitehead under the phenomenal reading, structural realism plus James under the functional reading. The subtraction audit's line: "nothing distinctive remains." Classification: COHERENT BUT NOT DISTINCTIVE. Coherence is necessary, not sufficient; the explanatory ambition was found unearned.

## RD-003: familiar concepts, no new bridge

The generality chain — shared perspectives → generality → imbalance → selection → persistence — broke on four of six links, each break requiring time, memory, aggregation, or dynamics the framework does not contain. The central inference (generality favors actualization) proved circular or unsupported; reverse causation (persistence *producing* apparent generality) fit better. Every concept located in prior literature: Kitcher/Goldman (consensus), Whitehead (prehension), Tarski/Mac Lane (generality). Classification: FAMILIAR CONCEPTS, NO NEW EXPLANATORY BRIDGE.

## RD-004: the portfolio decision

Separating the three layers, the final review judged [decisions reported as decisions, not theorems]:

- **A. The metaphysical commitment** — COHERENT; NOT FALSIFIED; NOT DERIVATIVELY FRUITFUL. Stipulations cannot be false; this one also proved barren.
- **B. The explanatory ambition** — UNSUPPORTED; SHOULD BE SUSPENDED. Structurally foreclosed by informational equivalence: physics contains identical information with or without posited sentience (I(T;S) = 0).
- **C. The research program** — SHOULD CLOSE. Every route tried and classified; the one positive output (Paper 3) is independent mathematics.

Classification: PHILOSOPHICAL FRAMEWORK RETAINED; EXPLANATORY CLAIMS SUSPENDED. The knowledge graph breaks at persistence→world (15 failed derivations); observer→experience fails on the combination problem; only relations→constraints→persistence derives — all standard mathematics.

## The payoff you were promised in the Prologue

You can now distinguish, on live examples: a *metaphysical perspective* (Chapters 1–6: coherent, stipulated, untestable), a *mathematical discovery* (Chapters 9–10: proved, checked, philosophy-independent), and a *scientific explanation* (missing throughout: no constraint, no mechanism, no prediction). Readers who internalize only one thing from this book should internalize this three-way split — it applies far beyond SGP.

## Where this chapter leaves you

Framework kept, claims suspended, program closed. Is that the end — no future, no hope, no next step? Chapter 21 states exactly what would have to change for research to honestly resume. The bar is high, and that is the point.
