# Chapter 16. Bose–Einstein Condensation and Collective States

**The image that tempts:** thousands of atoms surrendering individuality, flowing as one quantum wave. If anything in physics *looks* like SGP's unity themes, it is BEC. The physics is gorgeous. The analogy still isn't evidence.

## What BEC is [Established]

Some particles — *bosons* (photons, helium-4 atoms, rubidium-87 atoms) — may share a quantum state without limit, unlike fermions (electrons), which obey Pauli exclusion. Bose's 1924 counting and Einstein's 1924–25 extension predicted: cool a dilute boson gas far enough, and a *macroscopic* fraction piles into the lowest state — "macroscopic occupation of a single quantum state," described by one macroscopic wavefunction φ(x), the order parameter.

In 1995 it was seen: rubidium-87 at ~170 nanokelvin (Anderson et al., Science 269:198–201 — narrow peak atop the thermal distribution, abrupt growth on cooling, anisotropic minimum-energy profile), sodium the same year (Davis et al., PRL 75:3969). The condensate flows without viscosity, interferes like a single wave, and is genuinely collective.

## What BEC is NOT

It does not mean all matter becomes one object, or that constituents lose every distinction [Established]. Most atoms remain *outside* the condensate (the thermal cloud); the state is symmetric under exchange but not Bell-entangled in the subsystem-correlation sense; position measurements still find atoms *somewhere*. A condensate is one *mode* massively occupied, not the universe merging into a blob. (Figure 6 shows the level diagram: one rung crowded, the rest thinly peopled.)

The Institute's own records make exactly this distinction — to their credit: "IS BEC STRUCTURALLY SAME AS ENTANGLEMENT? YES: one joint state… NO: BEC has macroscopic occupation of one state; entanglement involves correlations between subsystems. VERDICT: BEC is a GENUINE case of the pattern. RELATED but DISTINCT" (sgp_physics_cases_01.py:271–276).

## Why BEC attracted the program — and where it stopped

Collective organization, common states, large-scale coherence: BEC is the best physical exhibit for "many becoming effectively one." But the exhibit never became an argument [Negative]: all physics-cases entries stayed Type 1 (genuine structural correspondence) with ZERO reaching Type 3+ (SGP adds constraint); BEC never even graduated to the bridge audit's candidate domains; measurement-threshold claims about BEC coherence were ruled "falsifiable but not SFH-testing" — matching data confirms standard physics, not SGP (T700.4).

A conceptual analogy motivates a question — "could stable shared structure emerge this way?" — but does not answer it. The book's rule from Chapter 5 holds: similarity is not mechanism.
