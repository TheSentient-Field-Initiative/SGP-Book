# Chapter 12. Can Interaction Explain Anything New?

**The temptation:** Surely *interaction* — things affecting each other, composing, competing, persisting — must add something beyond frozen relations. The Institute attacked this hope eleven times. Every attack ended the same way.

## The audit that closed the question

The Compass I boundary audit reviewed every prior assault on the Interaction debt — the interaction-primitive program, the response-first deep audit, the invariance audit, Exp24, Exp25, the C3 results, Road D, Stage 1 — plus the standard comparison frameworks, and tested six candidate distinctions between "arbitrary relation" and "interaction system." Classification: NO QUALIFYING MATHEMATICAL QUESTION FOUND. Three of seven stop conditions fired: all candidates answered by established mathematics; novelty evaporating under standard terminology; every result a restatement, routine corollary, or reclassification.

The audit's honest sentence deserves quoting in full [Negative]: the Interaction debt "is not blocked by a missing question. It is blocked by the fact that the questions that can be precisely stated about interaction have already been answered by existing mathematics — and the questions that cannot be precisely stated without SGP-specific primitives are not mathematical questions at all."

Ordinary mathematics already represents interaction richly — relations, morphisms, composition, dynamical systems, process theories. The program kept offering new words for these; the audits kept translating them back.

## Selection: amplification is not choice

Three experiments form the selection triptych [Negative]:

- **Exp20 (chirality):** symmetric dynamics can *amplify* an initial asymmetry — but amplification is not selection. Starting exactly symmetric stays symmetric. A megaphone is not a judge.
- **Exp21 (actualization):** actualization without a selector is generic mathematics — a differentiator plus instability suffices. Something happens rather than nothing for entirely ordinary reasons. Actualization is ∃ (existence), not ∃! (unique choice).
- **Exp19 (plurality):** multiple internally coherent models coexisting is generic mathematics with zero SGP residue. Plurality is the default; convergence needs explaining, not divergence.

And the positive searches failed: the selector's source is UNRESOLVED (selector audit :83); viability dynamics (coherence + stability + fertility) was never validated — all 7 kill tests unresolved; composition of sentient processes turned out generic (non-sentient processes do the same — SAO-1). A late proposal (shared perspectives → generality → imbalance → selection) broke on four of six links and recycled familiar philosophy (RD-003; see Chapter 20).

## What "no" means here

Not "interaction is meaningless" — the audit explicitly refuses that reading. It means: the *tested attempt* to derive distinctive mathematics from interaction yielded standard mathematics. Interaction remains real, rich, and studied — by the ordinary sciences of dynamics, which need no SGP primitive.

## Where this chapter leaves you

 mathematics could not deliver the bridge, perhaps *physics* could: even standard mathematics might constrain which physical structures are possible. Chapter 13 tests that hope — with the program's strictest gates.
