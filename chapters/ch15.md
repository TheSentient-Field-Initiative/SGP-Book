# Chapter 15. Entanglement and the Meaning of Separateness

**The question everyone asks:** Does quantum entanglement show that "everything is really one" — and does that prove SGP? The physics is real and astonishing. The inference to SGP is not licensed. This chapter separates them.

## What entanglement is [Established]

A quantum state describes a system by a vector (think: an arrow encoding all measurable tendencies). A *joint* state of two systems, |Ψ⟩_AB, can be *entangled*: it cannot be factored into "state of A times state of B." Measure A, and the outcomes correlate with measurements on B in ways no pre-arranged classical plan can mimic. The reduced state of A alone — ρ_A, obtained by the *partial trace* over B — is a projection: a shadow of the whole, not a complete description of a part.

In 1964 Bell proved the point mathematically: models satisfying locality plus hidden variables must obey inequalities that quantum statistics violate (Bell 1964, Physics Physique Fizika 1:195–200). Experiments confirmed the violation — Aspect's 1982 tests, and definitively the 2015 loophole-free tests over 1.3 km of electron spins (Hensen et al., Nature 526:682–686; S = 2.42 ± 0.20 against the classical bound S ≤ 2). Local hidden variables are empirically ruled out.

## What the correlations do NOT do

They do not permit controllable faster-than-light communication [Established]. This is the no-signaling theorem: the marginal statistics at B are independent of which measurement was chosen at A. You can share randomness faster than light could carry it, but you cannot *send a message* with it. Every "quantum telephone" idea dies on this theorem. (Figure 5 diagrams the setup: correlated outcomes, crossed-out signal arrow.)

"Nonlocality" itself means different things in different interpretations [Established]: in collapse readings, something discontinuous happens at measurement; in Everett, there is no collapse and correlations are branch structure; in Bohm, there is genuine nonlocal guidance plus unobservable preferred frame. The experiments constrain models; they do not select an interpretation. Anyone who tells you Bell tests "prove" their favorite metaphysics is selling something.

## The verdict on the whisper

Entanglement does not prove universal consciousness, does not prove SGP, and does not establish that all objects share one mind. It establishes: joint states, nonfactorizability, Bell violations, no-signaling. Everything beyond that is interpretation — including SGP's. Interpretations are allowed. They are not results.
