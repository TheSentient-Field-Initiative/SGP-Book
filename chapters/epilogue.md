# Epilogue — The Questions We Carry Forward

You began with a feeling every honest person has had: the world is *too* intelligible. Equations written in solitude predict planets, particles, condensates. Why?

You have now watched one bold answer attempt the whole journey. Start from sentience. Build relations, orientation, closure, fibers, boundaries. Knock on interaction, selection, physics. Document everything, including the failures. End with a kept philosophy, suspended claims, and a closed program.

What did the journey buy you? Not a solution. Something better calibrated: SGP helped *articulate* the questions — possibility vs. actuality, correspondence vs. mechanism, interpretation vs. explanation, stipulation vs. result. These distinctions are now yours; they work on any theory, including the ones you love.

And the questions SGP did not answer remain genuinely open: why mathematics fits experience; why these structures and not others; what, if anything, actualizes possibilities. No one has answered them. The Institute's contribution is a map of where one serious attempt stopped — with the stopping points surveyed, dated, and labeled, so the next attempt starts further along.

Do not force triumph. The world is still intelligible, still unexplained, still waiting. Carry the questions carefully. That was always the assignment.
