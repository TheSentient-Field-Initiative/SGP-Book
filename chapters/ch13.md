## Interpretation vs. constraint vs. prediction

The chapter's discipline — the book's discipline — is a four-way split the records enforce:

- An **interpretation** of physics (a new way to read existing theory) — achieved repeatedly (Level A).
- A **mathematical restriction** on possible models — never achieved (B = 0).
- A **new explanatory mechanism** — never achieved.
- A **falsifiable prediction differing from alternatives** — never achieved (C = 0).

Interpretations are not worthless. But they are not explanations, and the program's ambition required explanations.
