## A note on artificial intelligence

Readers may wonder what SGP says about AI minds. The honest answer is: almost nothing as a *result*, and something clarifying as a *lens*. Under the functional reading ("sentience = ability to respond"), any coupled system — thermostat, chess program, language model — trivially "responds," which shows how little the functional definition discriminates [Author-inference]. Under the phenomenal reading ("capacity to feel"), nothing in the records operationalizes feeling, so no AI system (or brain, or thermostat) can be classified either way. The program's contribution to AI is therefore the same three-way discipline as elsewhere: do not confuse functional interaction (which AI clearly has) with phenomenal sentience (which the records never made testable) or with an explanation of either. No AI-specific bridge, constraint, or prediction appears in the records.

## The pattern across all frontiers

Every contact follows one shape: genuine open problem → SGP relational reading (Level A) → bridge attempt → gate failure (arbitrary encoding, added parameters, or representation-relativity) → closure. The five phenomena of the hard-gate screen (entanglement, singularities, fine-tuning, unreasonable effectiveness, consciousness) are "standard physics open problems" whose unresolved status "ordinary physics already provides" — no monopoly, no lever (STE screen).

## Where this chapter leaves you

Physics, surveyed honestly, gave the program interpretations but never a constraint. Part IV now does the hardest narrative job: telling the negative results as the intellectual story they are — without burying them, and without mistaking them for a disproof.
