# BOOK — Master Entry Point

**Working title:** *The Sentience Question: From the Foundations of SGP to the Mathematics of Reality*
**Subtitle:** *What We Discovered, What We Could Not Derive, and the Search for Why This World Exists*

## How to read

Chapters are the narrative (general reader, no advanced training assumed). Appendices are the audit trail (every principal claim traceable). Evidence labels ([Established] … [Unresolved]) are defined in `appendices/M-evidence-key.md` and used consistently. Figures are illustrations, never evidence.
