## The five-commitment package (RD-001 §2, verbatim :345–349)

- **C1:** "Sentience means the ability to respond (a functional definition of sentience, not a phenomenal one)." (:345)
- **C2:** "Reality is sentience (an identity claim: the fundamental nature of what exists is the capacity to respond)." (:346)
- **C3:** "The physical universe is experience-content (a constitutive claim: physical objects are what sentience contains, not what sentience is caused by)." (:347)
- **C4:** "Mathematics describes possible structures (a modal claim: mathematics is about what *could* be, not what *is*)." (:348)
- **C5:** "Some possibilities become actual (an actualization claim: the transition from possibility to actuality is the fundamental process)." (:349)
- Architecture (:351): C2 needs C1; C5 needs C4; C3 needs C1+C2. Distinctive moves: category move C1→C2 (:355); modal move C4→C5 (:357).
