# Appendix M — Claim Classification and Evidence Key

Labels used consistently throughout the book (shown in brackets after claims):

- **[Established]** — Settled science or textbook mathematics (e.g. Bell violations; symmetrization; 2^(n²) census arithmetic).
- **[Math-verified]** — Proved in the records (theorems, audit reasoning). Manuscript proofs are marked "-as-manuscript" with peer review outstanding.
- **[Finite-check]** — Exhaustively computed within a stated finite scope (|X|≤4; |S|≤5), not a general proof.
- **[SGP-commitment]** — Foundational stipulation or Institute position (C1–C5; governance question). Not testable; can be coherent or incoherent.
- **[SGP-interpretation]** — The Institute's reading of standard material (Level A). Allowed; adds no constraint.
- **[SGP-hypothesis]** — Speculative proposal under investigation (e.g. ONE-EQUATION pattern before downgrade). Testable in principle.
- **[Author-inference]** — This book's own synthesis, arithmetic, or framing. Checkable; not attributed to the records.
- **[Negative]** — Falsified, bounded, or closed-against (R35; Exp24/25 verdicts; Road D). Includes "absent from records" search results.
- **[Unresolved]** — Open with no current path (selector source; viability; locality driver) or open-with-path (Appendix L).

Combinations occur (e.g. "[Established] + [Negative]" where the records confirm standard physics and close the SGP add-on). The label travels with the claim in every chapter.
