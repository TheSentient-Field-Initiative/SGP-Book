# Appendix A — The Raw SGP Vocabulary

Glossary. Each term: book formulation, original wording, source, date, revisions. Source: research-map 03-terminology-map.

- **Sentience (phenomenal).** Book: the capacity to feel, as stipulated starting point. Original: "Sentience (the capacity to feel) is the starting point of the research program, not a hypothesis to be proved." (GOVERNANCE.md:19; frozen 2026-07-26.) Glossary repeats (docs/glossary/glossary.md:167).
- **Sentience (functional).** Book: the ability to respond (to another or itself). Original: "Sentience means the ability to respond (a functional definition of sentience, not a phenomenal one)." (RD-001:345; 2026-10-07.) Also: "SENTIENCE = ability to respond; response = ordered pair membership" (Stage1:260).
- **Sentience (short/unqualified).** "Sentience is the starting point, not a hypothesis to be proved." (README.md:13; git 2026-05-19.)
- **Sentience (first-person).** "Treated as self-evident first-person reality." (RD_RESEARCH_COMPASS.md:34; git 2026-06-14.) Superseded by governance wording.
- **Reality.** "Reality is sentience (identity claim)." (RD-001:346.) Earlier: "REALITY = SENTIENCE"; predecessor "Consciousness (sentience) is ground of being" (via RD-002:71–78).
- **Response.** Formal: response(R) ⟺ R≠∅. (Exp24:59–63.) Killed readings: verb-like primitive, capacity (Exp22:360).
- **Experience-content.** "Physical objects are what sentience contains, not what sentience is caused by." (RD-001:347.) Earlier: "the 'world' as experienced."
- **Mathematics (as possibility).** "About what *could* be, not what *is*." (RD-001:348.) Least controversial commitment (RD-002).
- **Actualization.** "Transition from possibility to actuality." (RD-001:349.) Frozen: without selection = standard math (differentiator + instability).
- **Selection.** Distinguished from actualization and amplification; source UNRESOLVED (selector audit :83).
- **Persistence.** "Remaining stable under perturbation… what survives repeated interaction." (glossary:129–130.) Independent property, not part of the primitive (Exp22:176).
- **Relation.** Set of ordered pairs; specification of which things connect to which. (Background.)
- **Invariant subset / family.** A ⊆ S with R(A) ⊆ A; the family Inv(R). (R-program.)
- **Closure system / operator; fiber; R_C; essential edge.** See Appendix D/I.
- **C1–C5 (philosophy).** RD-001 package (§2). WARNING: distinct from Stage-1 C1–C4 (math grammar: iteration, hierarchy, composition, rewriting).
- **SGP.** This book: sentience-first program. NOTE: `theory/SGP.md:1` defines an unrelated "Structured Geometry Program"; SFI/SFH/SGI identity unresolved (LOST_WISDOM:249).
